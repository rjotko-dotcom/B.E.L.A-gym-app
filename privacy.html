B.E.L.A — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

B.E.L.A is a personal desktop application written and used by one person, Rimvydas Jotko, to organise his own life data: finances, health, studies, tasks and notes. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its author.

1. Who is responsible

Data controller: Rimvydas Jotko, Lithuania. Contact for anything about data: rjotko@gmail.com.

2. What data the application handles

  • Bank account information — account balances and transaction history for the author's own bank accounts, obtained with the author's explicit consent through the PSD2 account-information service of Enable Banking Oy (Helsinki, Finland), an authorised provider supervised by the Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority.
  • Other personal data the author enters himself: tasks, habits, notes, study timetable, workouts and meals.

The application never has, asks for, or stores bank login credentials. Authentication always happens at the bank, using Smart-ID, Mobile-ID or the bank's own app. Access is read-only: the application cannot initiate payments or change anything at the bank.

3. Where the data lives

All data is stored locally on the author's own computer in Lithuania, encrypted at rest with the operating system's data-protection facilities. Bank data is not uploaded to any cloud service, analytics service or third party. Enable Banking acts as a technical intermediary between the bank and the application and, per its own terms, does not retain the account data it relays.

4. Purpose and legal basis

Purpose: the author's personal budgeting, net-worth tracking and record keeping. Legal basis: the explicit consent the author gives at the bank (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) and, for household use, the personal-use exemption (GDPR Art. 2(2)(c)).

5. Sharing

Bank data is not shared with anyone. No advertising, profiling or sale of data takes place.

6. Retention and withdrawal

Transaction history is kept for as long as the author keeps it, and may be deleted by him at any time from the application. Bank access consent is valid for the period set by the bank (typically 180 days) and can be withdrawn at any time at the bank, in the Enable Banking control panel, or from within the application; withdrawal stops all further data retrieval.

7. Rights

As the only data subject and the controller are the same person, all GDPR rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection) are exercised directly. Questions or complaints: rjotko@gmail.com; the competent supervisory authority is the State Data Protection Inspectorate of Lithuania (vdai.lrv.lt).

8. Changes

This page is updated when the application's handling of data changes. The date at the top shows the current version.
